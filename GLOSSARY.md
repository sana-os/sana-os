# Glossary

> **Developer reference.** Not part of any standard prompt loading order. It records what a name refers to, where it is defined, and what is still undecided about it.

## Time Layer

- **What it is now:** the name of a concept, not a file. There is no `Time_Layer.md`, and none is planned.
- **History:** Time Layer was once a framework of its own. It was later absorbed into other files.
- **Where it is defined:**
  - `frameworks/Legitimacy_Layer.md` §7 "Interaction with Time Layer" — time-lag crisis (ΔT) when institutional change outruns narrative absorption
  - `core/Integrated_Knowledge.md` §3 — absorption capacity = min(V_B, V_C); ΔT = V_A − min(V_B, V_C)
- **Checked and not found:** `frameworks/History_Analysis.md` was at first thought to carry part of it. On checking (2026-10-18) it does not mention Time Layer, ΔT or time lag.
- **References that remain:** `Communication_Layer.md` and `Legitimacy_Layer.md` list "Time Layer" under `related_frameworks`; `CPM.md` mentions "Future SANA OS layers (including Time Layer)"; `Persona_History.md` and `Persona_Template.md` name it among the lenses. These are kept as they are. A tool that resolves names to files maps "Time Layer" to the two places above.
